A little about me

Quality is a team practice.

I’m a Quality Assurance Engineer and SDET who enjoys making software dependable, understandable, and ready for real users. My work spans exploratory test strategy, manual testing, and automation for API, web, and mobile (iOS and Android). Also AI-powered features, which are fun to test and often require a different approach than traditional software.

I work closely with developers and product teams to find issues early, describe them clearly, and make quality checks part of the delivery process. I care about the details that make a product feel trustworthy as much as whether a test passes.

Outside work, I enjoy exploring testing tools, teaching QA, spending time with my family, and playing Dota.
